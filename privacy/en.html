Breakfree Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-28

In short

Breakfree has no sign-up, and nothing you write or record is sent to the developer. Everything stays on this device.

What we collect

The developer collects no personal information. The app uses no ads, no analytics, and no location, contacts, camera or microphone.

What is stored on your device

The tasks you write, your expected and actual scores, your smash records, and your settings (language, color theme, sound, vibration) are stored only in the app storage on your device. The developer cannot see them. They are deleted when you clear them in the app, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.

Export and sharing

If you tap export or share-as-image in the app, a records file or an image is created and goes only to the app or location you choose. Nothing is created unless you tap, and nothing goes to the developer. The records file contains what you wrote, so choose where you send it with care.

Heavy-words notice

If something very heavy is written, the app shows a short notice. This check happens only on your device, and the text is never sent anywhere.

Server that delivers the app files

The app screen files are delivered by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Children

Breakfree is not designed for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
